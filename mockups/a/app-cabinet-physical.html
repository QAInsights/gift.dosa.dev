Try it

  • Open the cabinet — the doors swing on their hinges; the drawer fronts settle in one after another.
  • Pull a drawer — click its front. It tips toward you, the tray opens, and the cards fan up. Click again to push it in; opening another drawer closes the first, and the rest of the cabinet dims.
  • Flip a card — click any card for its vault side; click again to flip it back.
  • Anniversaries is sealed — pull it and use the demo code 1234. A wrong code rattles the drawer front.

In the app, a flipped card carries the real actions (spend, vault, memory) and each front links to its manage panel. Every motion here is transform and opacity only, and all of it collapses under reduced-motion settings.
